# Donna.AI on Google: ready-to-paste profile

Owner does this once at business.google.com (it needs your Google sign-in and a verification code, so Jarvis can't do it for you). About 15 minutes, plus Google's verification wait.

## Steps

1. **Business name:** `Donna.AI`. Use exactly this. Google suspends profiles that add keywords to the name ("Donna.AI Ottawa AI Receptionist" is not allowed).
2. **Type:** "Service business: I deliver goods and services to my customers". Answer **No** to "Do you want to add a location customers can visit?".
   - Google still asks for an address to verify you. Enter the home address there. With no storefront, Google keeps it **hidden** and never shows it.
   - Check afterwards: Profile → Edit → Location → "Show business address to customers" must be **off**.
3. **Service area:** Ottawa, Kanata, Nepean, Orléans, Barrhaven, Gatineau. Use cities, not a radius; Google allows up to 20.
4. **Primary category:** `Business management consultant`.
   - Additional categories: `Marketing consultant`, `Software company`.
   - If one of these isn't offered, pick the nearest one in Google's list. Never invent a category.
5. **Phone:** the Donna demo line (the Retell number), so callers hear the product.
   - **Website:** the site address once the site is public. Until then, leave it blank.
6. **Hours:** Mon–Fri 9:00–17:00. Calls outside those hours still reach the AI on the demo line.
7. **Description:** paste the block below. It is under 750 characters, with no links and no prices, per Google's rules.
8. **Services:** paste the list below (Profile → Edit → Services).
9. **Photos:**
   - logo (square);
   - cover image (the site hero);
   - 3 to 5 screenshots: the leak calculator, a sample text-back, the call summary.
   - **No stock photos of people presented as staff or clients.**
10. **First post:** paste the Offer post below. The free-setup deadline is real (Dec 31, 2026). Delete the post on Jan 1.
11. **Reviews:** only real clients, asked after a real result.
    - Never offer anything in exchange for a review.
    - Never post reviews yourself, or have friends post them for a business they haven't used. That breaks Google's policy and the Competition Act.
