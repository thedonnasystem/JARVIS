# Playbook: generic local service

Positioning: one clear promise tied to the offer, for the stated target customer in the stated service area. Lead with the customer's problem, then the offer, then one low-friction next step (book a call / get a quote).
Audience angles: (A) problem-aware (they feel the pain now), (B) solution-shopping (comparing providers), (C) trigger moment (a seasonal or life event that makes the offer timely). Describe audiences in words only (interests, situations, places as stated); never personal data.
Ad variants: per angle one short direct ad, one story/empathy ad, one proof-or-process ad (how it works in three steps; no invented proof).
Creative: real-looking photos of the service context, no faces of real identifiable people, no fake testimonials. Every AI image or video is labelled as AI-generated where the platform requires it.
Follow-up: touch 1 within minutes (SMS), then email next day, SMS day 3, email day 6, final SMS day 10. Every SMS names the business and ends with "Reply STOP to opt out". Every email has the business name and an unsubscribe line. Only send after consent is recorded.
Organic: 14 days, mix of tips, behind-the-scenes, FAQ answers, local relevance, and soft calls to action (no more than 1 in 3 posts asks for the sale).

## Confirm with the client
- The exact business name, licence or registration details (if any) and how they must appear in ads and on the landing page.
- The exact consent wording shown next to the lead form, and who keeps consent records (CASL: express consent, sender identification, unsubscribe honoured within 10 business days).
- The service area exactly as the client wants it described; no area is assumed.
- Who approves ad copy and creative before anything is published, and how.
- A privacy notice for the landing page (how lead data is used, kept and shared).
- Whether any claim in the drafts needs proof the client can supply; remove it if not.
