# Playbook: insurance (life / living benefits professional)

Positioning: protection and peace of mind explained simply by a licensed professional; education first. Good angles: young family protection, mortgage protection planning (no amounts), business owners' coverage review, "review what you already have".
Never promise returns, guaranteed growth, "tax-free wealth" outcomes, premiums, or approval. Never ask about health, medical history, smoking, age or income. Do not name specific products or insurers unless the brief names them.
Lead form: name, email, phone only.
Every ad and the landing page carry a placeholder: [CLIENT TO CONFIRM: licensed name, licence details and agency/MGA as required].
Recruiting (agent opportunity) ads are NOT part of this pack unless the brief explicitly says so; income claims are never made.
Creative: families, everyday moments, conversations; no hospital or medical imagery.

## Confirm with the client
- Provincial insurance advertising rules (e.g. FSRA in Ontario, conduct and fair treatment expectations): licensed name and any required disclosures; no misleading statements about coverage, cost or returns.
- No guaranteed returns or investment-style claims for any insurance or segregated fund product.
- Meta special ad category: confirm whether the ads fall under a restricted category (credit / financial products) in Meta's current policy and plan targeting accordingly.
- CASL consent wording and consent records; no follow-up before consent and the owner's outreach gate.
- The agency/MGA's own approval process for ads.
- No sensitive personal information is ever collected through the agency's forms or AI; the client handles it in their own systems.
