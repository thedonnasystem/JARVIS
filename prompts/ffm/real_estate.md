# Playbook: real estate (agent, team or investor-buyer)

Positioning: local knowledge and a clear next step (home valuation conversation, buyer consultation, quick sale option) as stated in the brief. Good angles: sellers thinking about timing, buyers new to the area, owners who want a simple/as-is sale.
Never promise a sale price, timeline, "we buy any house for cash at top dollar", or market predictions. No claims about neighbourhood demographics; describe places, not people.
Lead form: name, email, phone only (no property address or financial questions in this form).
Every ad and the landing page carry a placeholder: [CLIENT TO CONFIRM: brokerage name and registration details as required].

## Confirm with the client
- Real estate advertising rules in the client's province (e.g. RECO / TRESA in Ontario): brokerage name shown clearly, registrant names and titles as registered, no misleading claims.
- Meta special ad category: housing ads targeting Canada must be declared and have restricted targeting (no age/gender narrowing, no lookalikes, minimum location radius). Confirm in Meta's current policy.
- Fair housing and human rights: no audience or copy that excludes or prefers people by protected grounds.
- CASL consent wording and consent records; no follow-up before consent and the owner's outreach gate.
- Any sold/price/record claim must be provable by the client or removed.
