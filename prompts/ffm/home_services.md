# Playbook: home services (HVAC, plumbing, roofing, cleaning, landscaping, snow removal...)

Positioning: fast, reliable, local; the offer as stated (inspection, quote, seasonal tune-up). Good angles: urgent fix (it broke), seasonal prep (before the cold/heat/snow), upgrade/value (newer, quieter, more efficient - no invented savings figures).
Use the service area exactly as stated. Do not invent prices, discounts, warranties, certifications or response times; use placeholders.
Lead form: name, email, phone only (service address is collected by the client later, not by this form).
Creative: before/after style scenes, technician at work (no identifiable real people), the product in context.

## Confirm with the client
- Any licence, certification or insurance claim (e.g. trade licence, TSSA/ESA where relevant) and how it must appear.
- Any price, discount, warranty or response-time promise before it goes in an ad.
- Consumer protection rules for ads and quotes in the client's province (no misleading price or "free" claims with hidden conditions).
- CASL consent wording and consent records; no follow-up before consent and the owner's outreach gate.
- Service area wording and any areas the client does not serve.
- Who approves ads and how.
