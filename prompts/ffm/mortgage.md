# Playbook: mortgage (Ontario brokerage or agent)

Positioning: help, clarity and speed of a licensed professional, never a rate or an outcome. Good angles: renewal coming up, first home planning, switching lenders at renewal, self-employed or newcomer guidance (described respectfully, without asking for financial details).
Do not mention or promise rates, payments, approval, pre-approval, "no credit check", savings figures or "lowest/best rate". Do not ask about income, credit, debts or down payment amounts. The CTA is a conversation (book a call), never "apply".
Lead form: name, email, phone only. The form must not ask about income, credit, property value or employment.
Every ad and the landing page carry a placeholder for licence display: [CLIENT TO CONFIRM: brokerage name and licence number, agent name, title and licence number as required].
Creative: homes, keys, calm conversations; no fake documents, no rate tables.

## Confirm with the client
- FSRA advertising rules (Ontario): the brokerage's authorized name and licence number shown clearly and prominently; agents show licensed name, approved title and the sponsoring brokerage's name and licence number; no team names in ads; nothing misleading or incomplete. FSRA does not pre-approve ads: the brokerage / principal broker reviews them. Confirm exact wording and placement for each ad format, including small mobile units.
- Meta special ad category: credit and housing ads targeting Canada must be declared and have restricted targeting (no age/gender narrowing, no lookalikes, minimum location radius). Confirm in Meta's current policy whether these ads are credit, housing or both before building audiences.
- CASL consent wording on the form, consent records, and that no follow-up is sent before consent is recorded and the owner's outreach gate is open.
- The brokerage's own compliance manual and approval steps (they override these drafts).
- Privacy notice for lead data (collection, retention, sharing).
- No documents, applications or financial information are ever sent to the agency or its AI: the client handles those in their own systems.
