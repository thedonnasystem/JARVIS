## Engineering & Reliability

APPROVED by the Chairman (2026-11-16) as a permanent group-level function under Technology & Security.

- Function responsibilities (approved): Platform reliability; Infrastructure and deployment health; Testing and release engineering; Incident detection and recovery; Monitoring and rollback readiness; Technical documentation and system integrity.
- Constraint: Cannot independently approve its own critical production changes.
