## 6. Review-request flow (real clients only) [S16]
1. **When:** after a real milestone, for example the first booked job from Donna, or day 30 live. Ask **every** client, not only happy ones. No review gating [S16].
2. **How:** a personal text or email with the review link (GBP → "Ask for reviews"). Draft:
   > "Hi [Name], it's Moe from Donna.AI. Thanks for being one of our first clients. If you have 2 minutes, an honest Google review would help other owners decide whether Donna fits them: [review link]. Good or bad, I read every one."
   - A client who gave you their number agreed to be contacted about their service. Send the request once, then one reminder at most.
3. **Never:**
   - pay, discount or reward for reviews;
   - write reviews for clients;
   - ask friends, family, staff or reps to review;
   - review competitors [S16].
   - The Competition Act also covers fake or misleading reviews [S14].
4. **Reply** to every review within 2 days, never with the client's private details.
5. **Do not** put review counts or stars on the site: the Web Studio validator blocks them.
