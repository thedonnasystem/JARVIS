## Legal notes for all options (not legal advice; have counsel review before use)
- **Guarantees, Competition Act s.74.01(1)(c)** [S10, S14]:
  - A guarantee is reviewable if it is misleading, or if there is no reasonable prospect it will be carried out.
  - Courts judge the **general impression**, not just the literal words.
  - Penalties reach $10M for a corporation [S10].
- **Performance claims, s.74.01(1)(b)** [S11]:
  - "Donna books you 3+ appointments" stated as a fact is a performance claim. It needs an adequate and proper test **before** you say it, and Donna has no client data yet.
  - So word every option as a **remedy you promise** ("if X does not happen, you get Y"), never as a prediction ("Donna will book you X").
- **Conditions travel with the promise.**
  - Show them in the same place, at similar prominence.
  - Fine print must not contradict the headline.
- **Words to avoid in client copy:**
  - "guaranteed results", "risk-free", "no risk", "100%";
  - "money-back" (unless cash really goes back);
  - any number of bookings stated as an expectation.
- **Keep records:**
  - the date each client accepted the terms;
  - their go-live date;
  - the booking log;
  - every credit given.
- **Honour it every time it triggers.** No case-by-case arguing.
- **Fit to B2B:** these are business-to-business service terms. Put them in the service agreement (`donna-launch` onboarding) as well as on the offer.

## Option A: 30-day booking promise (Capture and Command only) [OWNER: confirm]

> "If Donna.AI books fewer than **[N = 3]** appointments for you in your first 30 days live, your second month is free. Conditions below."

**Terms (draft):**
1. **Who:** founding clients on Capture or Command. Answer does not book, so it is not eligible.
2. **The 30 days** start on the go-live date: the day your call forwarding to Donna is switched on, after you approve the test line.
3. **What counts as a booked appointment:**
   - Donna puts an appointment into your calendar for a caller, and the caller gets a text confirmation.
   - Not counted: spam, duplicates, the same caller twice, reschedules of an existing job, and appointments you book yourself.
   - A booking counts even if the caller later cancels or does not show.
4. **Your part:**
   - Forwarding stays on for missed, busy and after-hours calls for all 30 days, as set at go-live.
   - At least **[10]** open slots a week are bookable in the calendar Donna uses.
   - Questions Donna needs answered are answered within 1 business day.
5. **Minimum call volume:**
   - The promise applies once at least **[30]** calls from distinct callers reach Donna.
   - If fewer arrive in 30 days, the window extends until they do, up to 60 days. After 60 days with fewer calls, the promise lapses. Say this up front: low volume is not Donna's to fix.
6. **The remedy:**
   - Month 2's invoice is credited in full, so you pay $0 for month 2.
   - It is a credit, not cash back. It applies only if you continue to month 2.
   - One time per client.
7. **How we measure it:** from Donna's booking log (call ID, time, calendar event ID). You get the log with the day-30 summary.

**N:**
- Set it at **3** [OWNER: confirm], or per client from the audit and calculator: about 25% of their estimated missed new-caller calls a month, capped at 5 [ASSUMPTION].
- Only offer this once the booking log is built and tested on Capture (the competitor research says to sell only what is built and tested [S15]).

**Exposure math** (revenue given up; the only cash cost is serving month 2):

| Scenario (10 founding clients: 7 Capture, 3 Command [ASSUMPTION]) | Clients who trigger it | Revenue given up | Cash cost (month-2 service) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Good fit, careful qualifying | 1 (10%) | $797 to $1,297 | $60 to $150 |
| Base [ASSUMPTION] | 3 (30%): 2 Capture + 1 Command | $2,891 | $180 to $450 |
| Worst case: every client | 10 | 7 × $797 + 3 × $1,297 = **$9,470** | $600 to $1,500 |

**Upside:**
- The strongest closing line in the market for skeptics.
- Every credited client stays at least 2 months, so you get a second month to prove value.

**Risks:**
- Low-volume clients trigger it: hence the volume condition.
- Disputes over what counts: hence the booking log.
- The validator blocks the word on the site: keep it to offer sheets and conversations until you approve a site change.

## Option B: paid 14-day pilot, credited to month 1 [OWNER: confirm]

> "Try Donna.AI live on your line for 14 days for **$297**. Keep going and the $297 comes off your first month. Stop, and we switch it off: you owe nothing more."

**Terms (draft):**
1. **Price:** $297 CAD + tax for Answer or Capture. **$497** for Command, which has more to set up [OWNER: confirm both].
2. **14 days live** on your real line, after you approve the test line. Your founding spot and founding price are held during the pilot.
3. **If you continue:** the full pilot fee is credited to month 1. Example: Capture month 1 = $797 − $297 = **$500**. Then the normal founding price, month-to-month.
4. **If you stop:**
   - Tell us by day 14. We switch forwarding off within 1 business day and send your call summaries.
   - The pilot fee is not returned (state that in plain words in the agreement), and nothing more is charged.
5. One pilot per business.

**Exposure math:**
- **Received:** $297, paid before setup starts.
- **Spent:** setup time of about 4 to 8 hours [ASSUMPTION], plus 14 days of cost-to-serve, about $30 to $75.
- **If the client stops:** cash stays positive (about +$220 to +$270). The loss is your setup hours.
- **Pilot-to-paid:** assume 50 to 70% [ASSUMPTION: no data].
- **Across 10 pilots at 50%:** 5 paying clients, plus $1,485 kept from the 5 who stopped. Setup hours spent on non-payers: about 20 to 40.

**Upside:**
- It qualifies buyers: anyone who pays $297 is serious.
- It turns "I need to think about it" into a small yes.

**Risks:**
- A lower first-month payment.

- 14 days may be too short for low-volume shops. Use the audit to pick who gets offered a pilot.
