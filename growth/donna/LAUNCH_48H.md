# Donna.AI: 48-hour launch plan and first 14 days

Labels: `[S#]` = [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md). **[ASSUMPTION]** = planning number. **[OWNER: confirm]** = your call.

**Headline:**
- In 48 hours, put the demo line and a real audit result in front of **every warm contact plus about 100 owners across the 7 verticals** (split by fit, [CHANNELS.md §2](CHANNELS.md)).
- Book 5+ audit reviews. Close the first client from the warm network or the first audit reviews.
- Then hold the **same daily activity volume** for 14 days. The vertical split changes only at the Day 7 and Day 14 checkpoints, and only on tracker data. Volume never drops.

**Who does what:**
- **You:** you are the closer for the first 3 to 5 clients.
- **`build/donna-launch` (the other agent):** site hosting and contact form, live demo line, audit-call/walk-in/closing playbook, offer sheet, onboarding.
- **This kit:** every other channel ([CHANNELS.md](CHANNELS.md)), the presence kit, outreach drafts, the calculator page, risk-reversal options and the tracker.

## Honesty first: nobody can guarantee clients
No plan, script or tool guarantees a paying client in 48 hours or 14 days. What you control:

1. **Volume.** The pipeline math below decides more than any script. Effort that does not turn into conversations does not count.
2. **The offer.**
   - Founding pricing with setup included, month-to-month, a free missed-call audit and a test line.
   - The competition mostly hides prices, charges setup, or locks clients into 12 months [S15].
3. **Risk reversal.**
   - Live now: the audit plus a test line before any customer hears Donna.
   - A stronger promise needs your sign-off first: options in [RISK_REVERSAL.md](RISK_REVERSAL.md).
4. **Proof.** You have no case studies yet, and you must not invent any. Your proof is:
   - the prospect's **own** missed call (the audit);
   - the **demo line** they can call right now;
   - **demo-call clips**, labelled as demos;
   - client #1's results, published only with their written permission.
5. **Speed.** Answer every inbound lead in minutes: the first responder often wins [S2, citing LeanData]. Forward your own missed calls to Donna, so you practise what you sell.
6. **Asking for the money on the call.** Every audit review ends with an offer and a payment link, not "I'll send you something".

## Pipeline math: activity needed for 1, 3 and 10 clients

**Stage rates [ASSUMPTION] until your day-7 numbers replace them:**

**How to read it:**
- Each row is the volume you need, by stage.
- Sales cycles add 2 to 7 days after the conversation [ASSUMPTION].
- Warm-network and partner conversations convert better than cold ones, so every warm conversation counts for about 2 cold ones [ASSUMPTION].
- **The 10 founding spots will not all fill in 14 days on your own unless the base rates hold or beat.** That is fine: the spots stay open only while they are real, and new clients pay the standard price once they are filled [S17].
- At about 3 hours a day (12 businesses a day), double the "days" column.

**The single metric to watch daily:** owner conversations. If you have fewer than 10 a day for 3 days, nothing else matters until you fix it: more dials, better call windows, more walk-ins.

## What to do when...
- **A warm contact is in any vertical** (HVAC, construction, fencing, salon, real estate): pitch them if they take calls, with the hook from [outreach/VERTICAL_HOOKS.md](outreach/VERTICAL_HOOKS.md). Finance contacts are mostly introducers. Always ask for one introduction.
- **Someone says "send me info":** send the offer sheet (`donna-launch`) plus the demo line, and book a 15-minute review in the same message. See [outreach/OBJECTIONS.md](outreach/OBJECTIONS.md).
- **Someone asks for a guarantee:** today you can offer the free audit, the test line and month-to-month. Anything stronger is an owner decision ([RISK_REVERSAL.md](RISK_REVERSAL.md)). Do not improvise a promise on the phone.
- **Client #1 says yes:** take payment on the call, hand off to onboarding (`donna-launch`), and put the referral ask on the calendar ([outreach/REFERRAL_ASK.md](outreach/REFERRAL_ASK.md)).
