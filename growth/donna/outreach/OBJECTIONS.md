# Objection handling: top 15

**The shape of every answer:**
1. Agree with what is true.
2. Answer in one or two sentences.
3. Move to proof: their audit, or the demo line.
4. Ask a question that moves to the next step.

**Never:**
- invent results, client counts or reviews;
- promise bookings;
- improvise a guarantee. Approved options only: [../RISK_REVERSAL.md](../RISK_REVERSAL.md).

| # | They say | You say |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | "My customers won't talk to a robot." | "Some won't, and that's fine. Donna says upfront she's an AI assistant, keeps it short, and if they want a person she texts you their details right away. Call the demo line now and ask for a real person, and see what happens. Would your customers rather get that, or voicemail?" |
| 2 | "I answer my own phone." | "When you're free, you do. What happens when you're with a customer, on a job, driving, or on another call? Last [day] at [time] your line [AUDIT_LINE]. Donna only takes the calls you can't, if you set it up that way." |
| 3 | "It's too expensive." | "Fair question. What's your average job worth? [X]. Capture is $797 a month at the founding price, so it covers its cost if it wins back [797 ÷ X] jobs a month. Want to run your own numbers on the calculator together?" |
| 4 | "I already have an answering service." | "Good, then you know the value. Three checks: does it book into your calendar, text back callers who hang up, and follow up leads that don't book? If yes, keep it. If it just takes messages, Donna does more for a published, flat monthly price." |
| 5 | "I don't miss many calls." | "You might be right. Want to find out for free? We call your line during the day and after hours like a new customer and show you what happened. If it's all answered, I'll tell you not to buy." |
| 6 | "My customers are older." | "Then patience matters. Donna speaks slowly and clearly, repeats things, and hands off to you whenever someone asks. You approve every word on a test line first. Want to hear the demo with an older caller?" |
| 7 | "What if it books the wrong job or gives a wrong price?" | "You set the rules: which jobs, areas, hours and prices. Anything outside them becomes a message to you, not a booking. And nothing goes live until you've tested it and said yes." |
| 8 | "I tried software like this and it was a pain." | "Most tools make you build and tune it yourself. We do it for you: we set it up, you test it on a private line, we keep it tuned. Month-to-month, so if it's a pain, you stop." |
| 9 | "Is there a contract?" | "No. Month-to-month. Setup is included for the 10 founding clients, and the founding price stays locked while you stay." |
| 10 | "I need to think about it." | "Of course. What's the one thing you'd want to be sure of? [Answer it.] Founding spots are limited to 10; I'd hate for you to lose the price. Can we book 10 minutes on [day] to decide?" If approved, offer the paid pilot. |
| 11 | "Send me some info." | "Will do: the one-page offer and the demo line. Most owners decide after hearing it, so let's put 15 minutes on [day] to look at your audit together. [Day] or [day]?" |
| 12 | "You have no reviews or clients." | "True, we're new, and I won't pretend otherwise. That's why the founding price exists, and why you get a free audit and a private test line before you pay or go live. Month-to-month, so the risk is one month." |
| 13 | "What about privacy and call recordings?" | "Callers are told it's an AI assistant. Call details go to you and are used to answer and book the job. We'll show you exactly what's stored and for how long before you go live." [OWNER: confirm the data and retention policy with counsel.] |
| 14 | "Can it handle [complex question]?" | "Let's test it. If it can't answer, it takes a message and texts you a summary, so the caller isn't lost. Call the demo line now and ask it that exact question." |
| 15 | "I'm too busy right now." | "That's exactly when calls get missed. It's 15 minutes, and we do the setup. Is early morning or after 5 better?" |
