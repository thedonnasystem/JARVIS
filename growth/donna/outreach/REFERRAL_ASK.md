# The referral ask for client #1

**When:**
- Right after a real win: the first job Donna booked for them, or the day-14 check-in once calls are flowing.
- Again at day 30.
- Never before go-live.

## In person or on the phone (the main way)
> "[First name], can I ask you something? You're my first client, and you've seen what Donna did this month: [true fact from their log, e.g. '6 after-hours calls answered, 2 booked']. Who are two other owners you know, in your line of work or close to it, who are always too busy to pick up and probably missing calls? I'll offer them the same free audit. I won't mention you unless you say it's OK."

**Then:**
1. Write the names down while you are with them.
2. Ask: **"Would you be OK sending them a quick text introducing me, or should I mention your name?"**
3. If they introduce you, follow up within 24 hours. Your first message names them as the referrer and carries your contact details and an opt-out (CASL referral rules [S9; VERIFY]).
4. Log `referral_asked_date` and `referrals_given`. Log the new rows with `source_channel = referral` and `source_detail = [client]`.

## Text version (they gave you their number as a client)
> "Hi [First name], Moe from Donna.AI. Glad the [true result] came through. Quick favour: know one or two owners who miss calls on jobs? Happy to give them the free audit. Totally fine if not."

## Referral reward: PROPOSAL [OWNER: confirm]
- **A $200 credit on the client's next invoice for each referred business that becomes a paying client** (after its second payment).
- **No reward for reviews.** Reviews and referrals are separate: a reward for a review is not allowed [S16].
- **Exposure:** a $200 credit per new client. Against a Capture client worth $797 a month, the credit is a quarter of one month.

## Asking for permission to use results (case study)
> "Would you be OK with me sharing your results on our site: [exact numbers]? I'd show you the exact wording first, and I can leave your business name out."

**Get it in writing** (an email "yes" to the exact wording). Until then the site's "Client results" slots stay empty, as they are now.
