# DM sequences (LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook): DRAFTS, nothing is sent

**Rules:**
- Treat every DM as a commercial message [S9; VERIFY how CASL applies to each platform's messages]:
  - identify yourself;
  - give an easy opt-out ("say no thanks and I won't message again");
  - message business accounts about their business only.
- **No mass DMs**, no automation tools that break platform rules, no fake accounts.
- Stop at "no". Mark `X` in the tracker.
- The demo line goes in the second message, or in the first if they asked.

## Instagram / Facebook: local business accounts (all 7 verticals; hooks in [VERTICAL_HOOKS.md](VERTICAL_HOOKS.md))
Before DM 1, follow them and react genuinely to 1 or 2 stories or posts.

**DM 1 (day 0):**
> Hey [Name], Moe here from Donna.AI. Saw your [specific post or job]. Nice work. Quick question: [OPENER] (No pitch if the answer's zero. Say "no thanks" and I won't message again.)

**DM 2 (day 2, or right after a reply):**
> Appreciate it. I built an AI receptionist for local businesses: it answers, texts back missed callers and books the appointment. Callers know it's an AI. Easiest way to judge it is to call the demo line: [DEMO LINE]. [DEMO_PROMPT] Tell me if your customers would hate it.

Where the app supports it, add a 20 to 30 second voice note or a clip of the demo call, labelled "demo call, not a real customer".

**DM 3 (day 6):**
> Also happy to run a free missed-call audit on your line: we call during the day and after hours like a new customer, and show you what happened. Want me to?

**DM 4 (day 13, last):**
> Last one from me. If missed calls ever get annoying, the demo line's open: [DEMO LINE]. All the best.
