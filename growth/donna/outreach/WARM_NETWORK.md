# Warm network: first 2 hours of the launch

**Who:** everyone you know personally who owns, runs or works in a service business. You have warm contacts in:
- HVAC;
- construction;
- fencing;
- finance;
- real estate;
- salons.

**How:**
- One by one, by text or Facebook Messenger: whichever you normally use with that person.
- Personal, short, in your own words. The drafts below are starting points: change any word you want.
- No group messages, no copy-paste blasts.

**CASL:** messages to people you have a personal relationship with are exempt [S9 FAQ; VERIFY]. Still:
- stop if anyone says no;
- do not add them to email lists.

## W1: owners in any of the 7 verticals (HVAC, construction, fencing, salons, real estate ...)
Use the `[OPENER]` and `[DEMO_PROMPT]` for their vertical from [VERTICAL_HOOKS.md](VERTICAL_HOOKS.md).
> "Hey [Name], it's Moe. Got a quick question for you as an owner: [OPENER] I just launched Donna.AI. It answers those calls, texts back anyone who hangs up and books the appointment. Call this: [DEMO LINE]. [DEMO_PROMPT] Tell me honestly what you think."

## W2: owners who may not feel the pain themselves
> "Hey [Name], Moe here. I just launched Donna.AI, an AI receptionist that answers your calls when you can't, texts back missed callers and books appointments. Call [DEMO LINE] to hear it. Two questions: do you ever miss calls on jobs or with clients? And do you know any owners who are always too busy to pick up?"

## W3: people in finance and real estate (introducers, sometimes buyers)
> "Hey [Name], hope you're good. I launched Donna.AI, an AI receptionist for service businesses. You know a lot of owners. Is there one business owner (trades, salon, clinic, law firm, auto shop, agent) you'd be OK introducing me to? I'll give them a free missed-call audit, no pressure. Demo line if you're curious: [DEMO LINE]."

## W4: anyone else
> "Hey [Name], quick favour. I just launched Donna.AI (AI receptionist for service businesses). Know any business owners who are always missing calls? A name would mean a lot."

## Second touch (day 2, to non-repliers; one only)
> "No stress if you're busy. If you get 60 seconds, call [DEMO LINE] and tell me if it sounds legit. Your honest opinion helps."

## When they reply
| Reply | Next step |
|---|---|
| They own a business and are interested | Call them today: a 10-minute conversation, then the audit and the founding offer (`donna-launch` closing script) |
| They give a name | Ask: "Would you text them first, or can I mention you?" Follow up within 24 h, naming them |
| "Cool, good luck" | "Thanks! If anyone ever mentions missed calls, send them my way." |
| Feedback on the demo | Thank them and log it. It's free product research. |
