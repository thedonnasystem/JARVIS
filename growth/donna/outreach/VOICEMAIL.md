# Voicemail scripts (live, left by you; never pre-recorded drops)

Under 25 seconds. Say the number slowly, twice. One voicemail per owner per week at most.

## V1: after an audit miss
> "Hi [First name], it's Moe with Donna.AI. I called [Business] at [time] on [day] and it went to voicemail, just like this one. That's actually why I'm calling. I've got a way to make sure those calls get answered and booked. Hear it in 60 seconds on our demo line: [DEMO LINE]. Or call me back at [OWNER: your number]. Again, [your number]. Thanks."

## V2: general
> "Hi [First name], Moe from Donna.AI. Quick one: [OPENER] We answer those calls for local businesses and book the appointment. Call the demo line: [DEMO LINE]. [DEMO_PROMPT] My number's [your number]. Thanks."

## V3: after a review no-show
> "Hi [First name], Moe from Donna.AI. Sorry we missed each other at [time]. I've got your audit results ready. It takes 15 minutes. Text or call me at [your number] with a time that works. Thanks."

## Your own greeting while you're prospecting (you'll miss calls too)
Forward your missed calls to Donna. Or, as a fallback greeting:
> "You've reached Moe at Donna.AI. I'm on a call. Leave your name and the best time to reach you, or call our demo line at [DEMO LINE] to hear Donna."
