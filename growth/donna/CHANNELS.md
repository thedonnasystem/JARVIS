**Top 5 for the first client, and why:**

1. **Warm network.** Trust already exists. There is no CASL issue with people you know personally. It is the fastest yes, and the fastest source of introductions to owners who do have the pain.
2. **Direct outbound with the audit.** It is the only channel where the prospect sees their *own* missed call before you pitch. That is honest proof while Donna has no case studies [S15]. The demo line lets them hear Donna in 60 seconds.
3. **Partners in each vertical** (supply houses, bookkeepers, brokerage managers, practice-management and legal-tech consultants). One partner can introduce you to 20 owners. It costs nothing, and they already have the trust.
4. **Local Facebook groups.** Trades, salon and small-business owners live there more than on LinkedIn (your earlier finding). Value posts open DMs without spamming. Real estate, law and clinics are the exception: LinkedIn works better for them.
5. **Cold email with the audit result.** Cheap and fills the calendar between call blocks. It is legal under CASL only with published addresses and a full footer [S9]. Average reply rates are low (3.43% [S3]), so it is a feeder, not the engine.

**Why not the others first:**
- Content, Google Business Profile, directories, podcasts and SEO compound. Rarely do they produce a client in 14 days with zero followers and zero reviews.
- Paid ads spend money before the message is proven.
- Start each of them now at low effort, because they make the top 5 convert better: a prospect who Googles you finds a real profile and real demo clips.

## 2. Verticals: run several in parallel, ranked by fit (hold the daily VOLUME steady, not one niche)

Donna.AI sells to any local business that loses money on missed calls. Run outreach across all 7 verticals at once, and split the effort by fit.

**Fit score** = call volume × missed-call pain × ability to pay × speed of decision. Each is scored 1 to 5, so the maximum is 625.

| Rank | Vertical | Call volume | Missed-call pain | Ability to pay | Speed of decision | **Fit** | Share of cold outreach effort | Warm network in it? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Home services: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, renovation | 4 | 5 | 4 | 4 | **320** | 30% | Yes: HVAC, construction, fencing |
| 2 | Real estate agents | 3 | 4 | 4 | 5 | **240** | 15% | Yes |
| 3 | Med spa, salons and beauty | 4 | 4 | 3 (med spa 4, salon 2) | 4 | **192** | 15% | Yes: salons |

| 7 | Cleaning and landscaping | 3 | 3 | 2 | 5 | **90** | 10% | Fencing and landscaping overlap |

**How the scores were set** [ASSUMPTION: all scores are judgment calls; replace them with tracker data]:
- **Home services:** the owner is on the tools, there are emergencies, and jobs are worth a lot.
- **Real estate:** agents decide alone and fast, but call volume per agent is lower.
- **Med spa and beauty:** busy hands, but many already use online booking.
- **Clinics:** high volume and ability to pay, but they have a front desk, privacy rules and two decision-makers.
- **Law:** high value per matter, but cautious buyers with confidentiality rules.
- **Cleaning and landscaping:** price-sensitive and seasonal.
- Hooks per vertical: [outreach/VERTICAL_HOOKS.md](outreach/VERTICAL_HOOKS.md).

**What to hold steady: the daily activity volume** (see [LAUNCH_48H.md](LAUNCH_48H.md)).
- The past pattern: med spas, mortgage brokers, agency owners and HVAC, 1 to 2 days each. The problem was never the niche. It was that no niche got sustained volume.
- Running verticals in parallel fixes the niche question. It does **not** fix volume.
- So the rule is: **the daily numbers never drop, whatever the split.**

**Rebalance only on tracker data, at fixed checkpoints:**

| Checkpoint | Look at, per vertical | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Day 3 | Audits run and owner conversations, in total | Under 60 audits or 15 conversations: the fix is more volume. Do not change the split. |
| Day 7 | Audit miss rate (share of lines with at least one miss) and conversation-to-review rate, per vertical | Move 5 to 10 points of effort from the 2 weakest verticals to the 2 strongest. Keep every vertical at 5%+ until day 14. |
| Day 14 | Reviews held and closes, per vertical | Pause a vertical only after 40+ contacts with 0 reviews held. Double down where closes happened. The total daily volume stays the same. |

### 4.5 Cold email under CASL implied consent (rank 5)
- **Legal basis [S9]:** implied consent from **conspicuous publication**, with three conditions:
  - the address is published, for example on the business's website or directory listing;
  - no "no solicitation" statement is published with it;
  - the email is about their business (their phone line).
- **Keep evidence** for every address: URL, a dated screenshot, and the date you checked.
- Every email carries sender ID, mailing address and an unsubscribe that works for 60 days [VERIFY]. Honour unsubscribes within 10 business days [S9]; do it the same day.
- **First actions:**
  1. Buy a separate sending domain [OWNER: confirm and buy] and set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
  2. Warm the domain up for 7 to 14 days before more than 20 emails a day.
  3. Write sequences from [outreach/COLD_EMAIL.md](outreach/COLD_EMAIL.md).
  4. **Send only after you have run the audit on that business**, so every email has a real, specific line in it.
- **Method for building your own list (no list is supplied here):**
  - Google Maps search "[vertical] [city]", split by the effort shares in §2;
  - open each website;
  - take the published email from the contact page;
  - check for a no-solicitation statement;
  - screenshot it;
  - log it in the tracker with `consent_basis = implied_published` and `consent_evidence`.
  - Skip personal Gmail addresses that are not published as the business contact.
- **Your time:** 30 to 45 min a day, 20 to 30 emails, hand-sent at first.
- **Cost:** domain about $15 to $25 a year plus a mailbox about $8 to $10 a month [ASSUMPTION]. Sequencer tool optional, $30 to $40 a month [ASSUMPTION].
- **Expected funnel per 200 emails:**
  - reply rate 3.43% average, 5.5% top quartile [S3]; with the audit detail, assume 5%, so 10 replies;
  - 4 positive replies (40%) and 3 conversations;
  - **about 0.2 clients.** It is a feeder.
- Follow-ups matter: step 1 brings only 58% of replies [S3].
